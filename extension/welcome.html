Welcome to Auto Translator

Chinese text is translated into English automatically: no clicks, free and right on this device. Only one short step is needed, once.

  1. Download the language packs

    Chrome downloads the Chinese → English and English → Chinese language packs once (traditional Chinese from Taiwan and Hong Kong is translated too). It takes exactly one click. After that everything works offline.

    Status: …

  2. Browse as usual

    As soon as the language packs are ready, Chinese text on every web page is translated by itself. Reload pages that were already open once. The extension icon turns translation off at any time, everywhere or for a single site.

  3. Text in images

    Chinese text in images, such as product photos and detail pictures in shops, is recognised and translated automatically too; the English appears right on the image. The “Original” button on the image shows the original. Right-click an image → “Translate image” to translate a single one. The text recognition also runs entirely on this device.

  4. Write in Chinese

    When you type into a field on a Chinese page, such as a message to a seller or a search, just type English. The Chinese translation appears below it; “Insert” or Ctrl+Shift+Enter puts it into the field. Your text stays on this device.
